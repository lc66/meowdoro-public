I'm done for today. You're not.
Keep working through the night. Follow the plan step by step, and run the tests after each one.
Split the work into stages you can stop after without losing anything. After each stage, check how much of my weekly limit is used. At 90%, stop: write where you stopped and what's next at the top of your notes, and wait for me.
Stop only if it's critical: something you can't undo, or a change to the plan itself. Then write the question at the top of your notes and wait for me.
Everything else, decide yourself and write down why.
Commit what works. Leave what doesn't on a branch, with a note.
In the morning, show me what you finished, what broke, and where to start, small enough to start with a coffee.
